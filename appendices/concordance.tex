\section*{Candidate cases from documentation-level review}

Two further reviews read project README files only, without inspecting implementations or running tests. A civic-maintenance verification design supplies a case for Chapters~\chref{20}, \chref{25}, and \chref{30}. A commerce system with stock reservations and a transactional outbox supplies a resource-commitment case for \Pref{P09}, \Pref{P20}, and \Pref{P27} (Chapters~\chref{31} and \chref{44}). An agent-automation tool bears on Chapter~\chref{24}. Thematic overlap with the book's concerns establishes no influence or borrowing, and promotional claims such as zero risk or general Sybil resistance are excluded.

\section*{Candidate imports from a source review}

A targeted review of material in the author's imported repositories identified four imports and several counterexamples. The review was not exhaustive, no files were copied, and implementation claims were not tested.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Replay integrity versus historical authority (Chapters~\chref{22}, \chref{25}, \chref{54}).
\item The identity $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P(\cdot\mid B)\,\|\,P)=-\log P(B)$ for restricted distributions (Chapter~\chref{16}), and the distinction between expenditure and damage to regenerative capacity (Chapters~\chref{7}, \chref{23}).
\item Allocation of finite maintenance capacity through explicit response functions (Chapters~\chref{17}, \chref{23}).
\item Conditional platform revenue $R(q)=(\alpha q+\beta)M(q)$ (Chapter~\chref{33}), restricted to the narrow revenue proposition.
\end{enumerate}

Counterexamples and exercises: a ledger verifier that does not recompute stored roots or aggregate repeated postings (Chapter~\chref{25}); a bounded-debt claim corrected by the cumulative-excess condition (Chapter~\chref{8}); sheaf repair claims that do not yield global sections (Chapter~\chref{58}). Claims about consciousness, universal entropy laws, cosmological foundations, or unverified performance are not imported.
