\chapter{Prerequisites}

The appendices supply background so that Parts~I to VIII need no outside reading beyond calculus and elementary probability.

\section{Optimization}
Convex programs, Lagrangian duality, Slater's condition, complementary slackness, and shadow prices (used for \Pref{P24}). Dynamic programming and the Bellman equation for the benchmark cases.

\section{Dynamical Systems}
Discrete-time systems, invariant sets, attractors, and thresholds. Continuous-time viability and Hamilton--Jacobi reachability~\cite{aubin1991,mitchell2005}.

\section{Probability}
Transition kernels, almost-sure events, measurability and selection, and the facts about Borel and analytic sets that \Pref{P25} requires~\cite{bertsekas1978}.

\section{Order Theory}
Partial orders, lattices, the Knaster--Tarski theorem~\cite{tarski1955}, order dimension, and multi-utility representations~\cite{dushnik1941}.

\section{Category Theory}
Categories, functors, monoidal categories, enrichment~\cite{lawvere1973}, resource theories~\cite{coecke2016}, and compositional models~\cite{fong2019,maclane1998}.

\section{Dependent and Linear Types}
Dependent types, linear and affine disciplines~\cite{girard1987}, session types, and refinement types.

\section{Sheaves}
Presheaves, sites, the sheaf condition, torsors, and \v{C}ech cohomology in degree one~\cite{maclane1992}.
