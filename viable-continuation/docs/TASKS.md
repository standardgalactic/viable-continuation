# Roadmap

Work in this order. Do not polish Part IX before Parts I to III exist as a connected argument.

## Phase 0: settle open decisions with the author (see NOTES.md)
- Add a benchmark chapter or appendix deriving a standard dynamic programme first, then other models under explicit specializations.
- Scope statement (money and banking, labour markets, trade, growth, mechanism design).
- Whether Parts X to XII become a second volume.
- Concordance sources for admissible degradation, continuous intervention, semantic frameworks.

## Phase 1: the housing case
Extend `frontmatter/housing.tex` and `docs/HOUSING_SPEC.md` through steps 1 to 4 of the spec (condition, obligations, accounts, shared crew). Add a companion check for each.

## Phase 2: Parts I to III (Chapters 1 to 15)
Write as decisions and trajectories. Prove P01 to P08 and P12 (a), P23 in their elementary forms inside the chapters that use them. Chapter 9 gets a full investment problem (P26 two-period form). Chapters 13 to 15 get the viability kernel, comparative statics, and the restoration certificate.

## Phase 3: Part IV (Chapters 16 to 20)
Valuation versus accounting versus implementation. Small allocation problem and multiplier (P24). Ledger, backlog, and restoration schedule side by side.

## Phase 4: Parts V to VIII (Chapters 21 to 40)
Own economic models per chapter. Develop P28 fully in Chapter 29 and P29 fully in Chapter 19 or 37. Ecology, care, development, and the transition chapter with a feasible path.

## Phase 5: Part IX (Chapters 41 to 45)
Rigorous consolidation. Fix statements first, then separate finite, countable, and general-state results. Resolve P25 general-state, P27, P12(b), P26 general.

## Phase 6: Parts X to XII (Chapters 46 to 60)
Category (full path category quotient, housing interfaces, functors with verified laws), types (judgments, semantics, preservation and progress, an expressible-but-not-executable commitment), sheaves (a record system that glues, a presheaf that does not, graph potentials before torsors).

## Phase 7: appendices, solutions, references, index
Prerequisites in full; worked solutions in `solutions/`; complete bibliography with primary sources; concordance with confirmed sources; notation index.
