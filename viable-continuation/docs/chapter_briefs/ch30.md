## Candidate import or correction already recorded

Candidate question: what must happen before a repair obligation may legitimately be discharged? A waiting period is an enforceable procedural condition but elapsed time cannot establish successful repair. Closure should require procedural authorization and specified evidence, with a route for reopening a failed repair. Grouping reports by location suppresses duplicate influence but does not establish independent witnesses, and a minimum cluster size does not by itself protect against all differencing attacks. Present as a proposed institutional design, not evidence of deployment.
